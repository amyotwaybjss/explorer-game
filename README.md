# explorer-game

A roleplaying game about explorers going into a mine to discover treasure. Information about the explorers is in a CSV file, stating their name, what date they began their expedition, what type of treasure they are collecting (gold, gems, artefacts) and whether or not they have specialised equipment (denoted by the date they purchased the special equipment) - for instance, a gem collector could find a ruby with normal equipment, or a ruby and diamond with special equipment. On a separate CSV, I have types of treasure, separated by type, whether they need specialised equipment to retrieve, and how difficult it is to find them. Difficulty is mapped to number of days required to find. I would like a simple app or webpage, ideally python or HTML based, for players to track their explorer’s treasure. They would be able to select their explorer from a drop-down, and depending on the information in the explorers CSV, it would display how many days they have been exploring (current date - start date), and buttons displaying the treasures available to them, based on their treasure type and whether they have special equipment. The player should then be able to select treasures based on their ‘time’ cost, until they run out of days. No treasure can be collected more than once. E.g. the earlier gem collector has been exploring for 6 days, so they could find a ruby (2 days), a topaz (1 day) and a diamond (3 days), but they could not bring back a ruby and three topaz.
